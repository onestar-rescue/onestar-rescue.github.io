What a fix looks like

One complaint, fixed three ways

Illustrative example for a fictional shop. Your kit uses your own reviews and your own sign-off.

Customer-update texts

For: “nobody called me with an update”

1) “Hi Dana, it’s Northgate Auto. Your Civic is checked in. We’ll text you by 11:30 with what we find.”

2) “Found a worn front wheel bearing. Estimate $412. Reply YES to approve or call us. Nothing extra gets done without your OK.”

3) “Your Civic is ready. Total $412, same as approved. Open till 6.”

Private win-back

Sent by the owner, not posted publicly

“Hi Marcus, this is Ray, owner of Northgate Auto. I read your review about not hearing from us on Tuesday. You’re right, that shouldn’t happen. We’ve started texting every customer at drop-off and before any work. If you’re open to it, I’d like to call you and hear what else we got wrong. – Ray, 555-0142”

Never conditional on changing or removing a review.

Calm public reply

For the unanswered review on your profile

“Marcus, thank you for telling us. Waiting all day without an update isn’t okay, and we’ve changed how we keep customers posted because of feedback like yours. I’d still like to make this right. Please call me directly at 555-0142. – Ray, Northgate Auto”

FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Do you need access to my Google account?

No. We work only from your public reviews. You paste the replies yourself and send the win-back messages from your own phone or email.

What if I don’t have many bad reviews?

Rescue One ($49) handles a single review. The Fix Kit works best with at least 3 low reviews in the last 12–24 months. If there isn’t enough to find a pattern, we’ll tell you before we start and refund you.

Is this only for auto repair?

Our scripts and texts are built for independent auto repair first: estimates, approvals, status updates, comebacks, pickup. HVAC and plumbing shops can use it too (missed arrival windows, surprise charges), with wording adjusted.

Will the win-back messages ask people to update their review?

No. They apologize, explain what changed, and offer to talk. Nothing is ever offered in exchange for changing or removing a review. That follows Google’s review policy and the FTC’s rule on consumer reviews.

What’s the “safe claim” in the competitor snapshot?

One true thing you can say in your ads or at the counter that addresses what customers complain about at nearby shops. We check it against your own reviews first, so it won’t backfire.

How do refunds work?

Rescue One and the Fix Kit have a 7-day money-back guarantee. Complaint Watch cancels anytime from your receipt link. See refunds.
